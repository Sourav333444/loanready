Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Short version: Your answers never leave your browser. We collect only your email address when you request your report. We do not sell it, share it, or track you.

What runs on your device

The SBSS score calculator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your answers to the 6 form questions are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server. We do not store your financial information anywhere.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you request your free report
  • Your role selection (Business Owner, Buying a Business, or Broker), so we can send you relevant information

That is it. Nothing else.

How we use your email

We send you a link to your PDF report. Occasionally we may send updates about LoanReady tools relevant to your role. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. One click and you are off the list permanently.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your email to anyone
  • We do not share your data with third parties for advertising
  • We do not run tracking pixels, ad scripts, or analytics tools
  • We do not pull your credit or access any financial accounts

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. We use browser localStorage briefly to pass your score from the calculator to the report page. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you close your browser.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts to load the Syne typeface. Google may log the font request. No other third-party scripts run on this site in the free phase. If we add Stripe for payments, Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment transaction.

Your rights
